Changes: Taken from the dataset's L1-normalised cubes (480x640x100); mean-binned 4x4 to 120x160 (2x2 in preprocessing, 2x2 here), L1 re-normalised per pixel, stored as float16. Labels: annotator 1 polygon mask, kept only where all source pixels are annotated.
